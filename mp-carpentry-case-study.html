01 · The Challenge
01

A skilled carpenter needed a website that looked the part.

MP Carpentry already had the experience and quality of work. The opportunity was to give that craftsmanship a stronger digital presentation and make the business easier for local customers to understand and contact.

The rebuild focused on clearer positioning, stronger visual hierarchy and dedicated routes into the services people are actually searching for.

02 · What Changed

A complete rebuild around trust and enquiries.

Rather than treating the website like an online leaflet, the new structure guides customers from first impression through proof, services and contact.

01

Premium visual direction

A more considered presentation that better matches bespoke carpentry and fitted furniture work.

02

Dedicated service pages

Individual pages for key services give customers more useful information and create stronger local search opportunities.

03

Mobile first contact

Clear call actions and a simpler mobile journey make it easier for prospective customers to get in touch.

04

Project gallery

The craftsmanship is given more room to sell the business visually instead of relying on claims alone.

05

Local SEO structure

Services and location are built into the information architecture so the site better reflects how local customers search.

06

Trust signals

Experience, workmanship guarantee and customer feedback help reduce uncertainty before someone makes contact.

03 · The Result
03

A site that feels more established, focused and credible.

The finished website gives MP Carpentry a professional home online that is much closer to the standard of the work itself.

The key improvement is clarity. A potential customer can quickly understand what MP Carpentry does, see the type of work available, explore relevant services and contact Matt without having to hunt around the site.

It also gives the business a stronger foundation for local search by separating major services into focused pages rather than trying to make one page do everything.
